# Find low-displacement intervals

Open a retained sampled-path account in **Sampled path**. After inspecting its
selected calibrated points, use **Find low-displacement intervals**. Supply a
maximum nominal spatial diameter and a minimum duration. The application finds
interval endpoints automatically and offers direct inspection of every original
point, marking the two points responsible for the interval's diameter.

The diameter constrains every pair of selected positions, not only successive
points. Thus a steadily drifting point cannot qualify merely because each small
step is short. The duration is the difference between the original endpoint
presentation timestamps. At least two known selected points are required;
missing selected observations split support. The result retains all maximal
qualifying intervals, including overlapping alternatives. It does not count
overlapping intervals as separate repetitions.

These are candidate dwells in sampled geometry. Large gaps can hide movement;
unselected moments, intervening motion, contact and intent are not inferred.
The source calibration, common plane and correspondences still require the
existing author declarations. A nominal diameter is not a measurement-error
bound or a physical stillness guarantee. This feature removes manual interval
endpoint selection, not initial point annotation.

Changing either bound clears the proposal. Editing the source declarations or
successfully opening another source retires it; a refused import preserves the
accepted work. Saving retains the complete original sampled-path account and all
original PNGs. Proposals are temporary and recomputed after reopening.
