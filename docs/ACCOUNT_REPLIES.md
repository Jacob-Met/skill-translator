# Receive an author reply to a comparison question

Open the complete original comparison and retain or reopen its field-specific
question. Under **Retain a supplied author reply**, choose the referenced example,
enter the supplied answer, declare its author/source and describe how it was
supplied. This records attribution; it does not authenticate the person.

**Explanation only** leaves every original account unchanged. To propose a changed
account, explicitly choose **Proposed account amendment**, enter replacement text
and confirm it. Only the questioned field in the chosen source step can change.
The original question, comparison and frame pixels stay unchanged. A separate
amended comparison begins with its global relationship unresolved; a previous
shared/different judgment does not silently carry over to different evidence.

**Withdraw unsupported optional account** explicitly removes a supplied visible-change, sensory or recovery note after confirmation, retaining the attributed answer and reason. The resulting field is not supplied — unknown; withdrawal is not evidence that a cue or change was absent. Required intent cannot be withdrawn, and an already-empty account cannot be withdrawn again. The original supplied text remains in the original comparison and reply inspection.

Save the reply separately. Reopen the original complete comparison, then its
question, then the reply. The receiver verifies the exact source/question and
recomputes the proposed amendment. Missing, changed or mismatched inputs refuse
without replacing the accepted reply. Editing the question retires the reply and
clears reply drafts. Editing a reply requires explicit retention again.

The two amendment download controls produce a separately amended original source
bundle or complete comparison, using the maintained PNG bundle writers. Preserve
the original files and reply alongside them to retain the amendment history. No
existing source is replaced automatically. The old question cannot reopen against
the changed comparison; make a fresh question for that new account if needed.

Reply files are bounded at 1 MiB, and source/comparison bundles retain maintained
capacity limits. No answer is sent to another person, author identity verified,
physical truth inferred, learner session synthesized or learning gain established.
This path is separate from clarification of an actual learner's retained practice
response, which remains in the existing decision-clarification consumer.

Native controls: `node test/account-reply.test.mjs`. Browser receiving:
`PUPPETEER_PATH=... CHROME_BINARY=... QA_OUTPUT=... node tools/browser-account-reply.mjs`.
