# Compare replies to one demonstrated-account question

In Demonstrated Decisions, open the original comparison and retain or reopen its
account question. Retain a supplied author reply in the existing panel, then use
**Add retained reply to comparison**. Retain another supplied answer and add it.
Two to six replies can be compared; identical repeated records are refused.
Multiple replies may have the same declared author. Names are not authenticated
identities, and repeated names or answers are not votes or independent evidence.

Each alternative retains its attributed answer, how it was supplied, selected
example, original account and explicit proposed replacement or withdrawal.
Explanations remain explanations; withdrawal means not supplied—unknown rather
than observed absence. The application does not infer semantic agreement,
contradiction, expertise or a preferred answer.

**Inspect this reply above** explicitly selects one original reply in the
maintained receiver. Its source/amendment download controls remain available;
selection neither combines proposals nor removes competing answers. Original
pixels and source accounts remain unchanged until a separate amended bundle is
explicitly downloaded. Keep original evidence and the comparison alongside any
derived bundle.

Save the comparison to retain a single shared question and all reply inputs.
Reopen the original complete source comparison, its question, then the reply
comparison. Exact source/question matching and a 1 MiB compact-file bound apply.
Displayed alternatives recompute; saved conclusions are not trusted. Changed
questions retire the old comparison, and late files cannot replace newer retained
replies. A single draft reply can be retained locally but cannot export as a
comparison until a second distinct reply is added.

Native controls: `node test/reply-comparison.test.mjs`. Actual browser receiving:
`PUPPETEER_PATH=... CHROME_BINARY=... QA_OUTPUT=... node tools/browser-reply-comparison.mjs`.
