# Compare demonstrated order

Open `/order-evidence`, import a saved demonstration as the reference, and add up to seven other saved demonstrations. Match each reference step explicitly to a comparison step. Titles and displayed step numbers help identify choices; matching labels never establish correspondence. Unmapped steps remain unavailable evidence. Exact retained IDs are preserved in the complete study.

The comparison uses intervals between retained before/after frame timestamps. If A's after frame is at or before B's before frame, that example records A at-or-before B. Otherwise the intervals overlap unless reverse separation is visible. These are not measured action durations or proof that a step completed successfully. A shared endpoint counts as at-or-before.

A candidate requires the same separated direction in every included example, including the reference, and at least two examples. Opposite recorded orders retain source witnesses instead of selecting a winner. Missing mappings and overlap remain unresolved. Renaming an identical retained frame/step record or reordering its equal-start step intervals does not make another example. Distinct accepted records are not claimed to be independent samples.

Inspect each witness with its exact original PNGs. An expert may select a stable candidate and supply a prerequisite reason. This declaration is separate from temporal evidence and uses the maintained prerequisite-rehearsal contract. Export the declared reference rehearsal and open it in `/prerequisite-rehearsal`, lock its declarations, then rehearse an order. Save the complete comparison study separately to retain every example, correspondence and selected declaration. Changing sources or correspondences clears declarations. An explanation still in the text box is a draft; use **Retain an explicit prerequisite** before saving it.

All accepted examples share a 32 MB PNG-data / 32 megapixel budget, at most eight sources, and a 48 MB serialized study limit. Source PNG admission is reused; saved derived findings are ignored. Reopen validates exact source relationships, mappings and declarations and recomputes results. Batch studies preflight aggregate limits before decoding. Complete import/adoption/cleanup operations are serialized, while edits invalidate pending work immediately. A replacement can temporarily coexist with previously accepted media; the retained-media budget is not a browser-heap limit. Saves must still match both the source epoch and accepted-state identity before downloading.

## Receiving

```sh
node --test test/order-evidence.test.mjs

```

The browser course uses the repository's Puppeteer environment, Chrome and ffmpeg. It makes two distinct original authored clips, captures them through the maintained Studio, downloads actual sequence bundles, maps and reverses correspondences, exports a declared rehearsal into its maintained consumer, and reopens the complete study at 390 px. It checks original PNGs, malformed/tampered sources, shared budgets, serialized superseded decodes, a delayed import after an expert edit, and an old-source save completing after replacement. These are software controls, not measured expert demonstrations. Exact executed source and results belong in the integration receipt.

This is guided investigation of observed chronology. It does not infer causal necessity, authenticate success, establish physical skill or authorize physical actions.
