# Discover relationships in a retained demonstration

From Demonstration Studio, save the original sequence with calibrated moments and open **Discover relationships across all calibrated moments**. In the existing relationship discovery workbench, select that one sequence bundle. Confirm corresponding mark IDs and a common fixed planar reference, give their basis, and choose the existing exploratory range and source category. **Discover from original moments** sends every calibrated moment through the maintained pair-enumeration mechanism.

This replaces exporting each moment's annotations and selecting 2–12 separate files. It does not require a candidate pair, relation kind, or a claimed invariant. Distance, horizontal and vertical hypotheses are still calculated by `relationship-discovery.mjs`. For example, the authored browser control has a pair rotating and translating across three original PNG moments: its 25 mm separation repeats while its directed components change. A new frame with doubled separation contradicts the distance hypothesis.

Every source card shows the original PNG and supplied point locations alongside its calibrated diagram. Requested/presented timing and source annotations remain in the original sequence. Uncalibrated moments remain saved but are explicitly named as outside the comparison. At least two calibrated moments are required, each with at least two marks under the existing discovery contract. All supplied reference-plane dimensions must agree. No label matching or automatic point identity is guessed. Missing corresponding marks stay incomplete under the existing inference mechanism.

**Save original inputs** retains the full original sequence, all PNG bytes, and declarations. Either the ordinary **Reopen saved investigation** control or original-sequence intake reopens that bundle and verifies original image identities before replacing accepted work. Derived findings are recomputed; altered PNGs refuse. Changing the exploratory range updates the saved declaration while preserving the source. Failed or superseded operations retain the accepted investigation.

The existing fixed/proportional family and pair-practice comparison consumers remain available. They receive the original calibrated annotations through their unchanged contracts; the full sequence and PNG bundle is retained by this discovery workflow and its export. Actual practice begins empty, with a separately declared tolerance. No source relationship is automatically adopted and no learner score changes.

These moments are samples from one clip, not independent successful demonstrations. The shared plane, calibration, correspondences and mark meanings remain supplied. Repeated geometry alone does not establish intent, contact, force, causal necessity, execution tolerances, physical success or learning. The controls are authored original-pixel exercises, not a physical craft trial.
