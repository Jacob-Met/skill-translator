# Learn to interpret retained original-frame evidence

The original edge-study consumer now leads to a bounded exercise: save the
study, open it in `/edge-decision`, choose a source step and declare familiarity.
Inspect the original before frame, retain a first prediction (including explicit
uncertainty), reveal the actual after frame and authored account, then retain an
evidence-linked reconsideration. The learner can request independent pixel
reports for either permitted anchor and inspect their original candidates.

This is an adapter to maintained `decision-practice.mjs`, not a new assessment
engine. That reducer continues to own first-response immutability, reveal order,
self-comparison, evidence anchors and meaningful reconsideration admission.
The adapter adds only source-edge disclosure and candidate-inspection events.
It produces no correctness, physical-skill, learning-benefit or mastery score.

## What remains hidden and what assistance means

Before reveal, the consumer creates no after image, authored step title/account,
after feature description, report count, thumbnail or aggregate study summary.
The setup uses neutral step numbers. The original before image is visible;
requesting its pixel report explicitly discloses the observer's selected,
rejected or unreviewed interpretation. None of these statuses means correct.
After evidence cannot be requested before the maintained source reveal. A
reveal before the first prediction remains visibly assisted after later answers.

Report requests and explicit candidate inspections retain their original moment
and segment identities and their interface order. Pre-answer timing is derived
by replay, never trusted from saved flags. Reopening restores the last explicitly
inspected candidate for each disclosed anchor. The initial default candidate is
shown as part of report disclosure; it is not mislabeled an explicit inspection.
At most59 candidate inspections reserve space for both disclosures plus the
complete prediction/reveal/reflection path within64 total actions.

The journal establishes interface order, not verified attention, authenticated
chronology or unseen performance. Familiarity remains a declaration. Starting
again creates a new account and cannot establish that a familiar source is
unseen. Portable source files contain their original evidence; this is not a
confidential examination system.

## Evidence the learner can inspect

Selected candidate support is recomputed by the original report admission and
merged complete-field helper. Cyan pixels show its retained support; the
original image remains visible underneath. The candidate line is a proposal.
Purple circles mark the existing sufficient internal-gap midpoint certificates.
No-gap does not certify continuity, and a gap does not establish physical edge
absence. Segment crossings do not establish contact. Independent moments do
not establish persistent feature identity or the motion between frames.

Missing reports stay uninspected and distinct from a zero-candidate report.
Rejected and unreviewed reports remain available with their original status.
The authored account is displayed as an account to reconsider, not an automatic
answer key. A reflection selects original before/after anchors and retains a
judgment or remaining question through the maintained v2 reducer.

## Source custody and receiving

The complete source study is frozen inside one exercise. Original PNGs are
checked through the maintained sequence receiver; all reports are admitted
against their exact source moments. Replacement adopts only after full
validation and starts a new exercise unless a complete saved exercise is opened.
A newer learner action or draft retires a delayed input. Invalid input preserves
accepted work. Saved exercises contain the original sequence/PNGs once, report
entries once and the raw bounded event journal; findings are recomputed.

A draft prevents saving until retained. A newer edit also retires a pending
whole-source export. Source replacement/page exit releases assets. The local
page has no network requests beyond its own static module assets, and image
raster overlays use its narrowly scoped `data:` image policy.
