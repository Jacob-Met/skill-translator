# Follow one fixed sequence of supplied cue readings

The existing fixed planner chooses the minimum count vector within its bounded family. Its ordinary Append/Inspect workflow intentionally computes another plan from the updated reading account. The fixed-execution workbench instead freezes one imported baseline and expands the chosen counts in original cue order. This order is declared, not optimized.

Save the complete plan in **Plan fixed repeated cue readings**, follow its execution link, and open that downloaded file. Original cue bundles and withdrawal accounts may also start a new sequence. Each supplied category must address the current fixed cue and retain an explicit origin and basis. Original-frame inspections retain the exact example, moment and PNG hash. Hypothetical answers remain hypothetical.

A plan may use fewer positions than its declared ceiling: the received scissors course uses three positions inside a six-reading ceiling. After those three positions the sequence ends; opening, answering or replaying never refills it. Original inputs, all baseline corrections/withdrawals, and supplied answers are saved. Reopen recomputes the plan and every answer account instead of trusting saved conclusions.

The same TOTAL mismatch allowance covers active baseline and new readings. Unknown source cells stay compatible without confirming an answer. A completed schedule separates represented decisions only while at least one retained example fits; answers outside that evidence stop execution. The workbench does not establish physical reliability, error independence or a probability model. It does not recommend an action or optimize effort.

The unchanged history reducer permits 30 introduced reading identities and 90 events. Withdrawn identities still occupy that retained limit. The entire selected sequence must fit the remaining capacity before execution is offered. No original history is dropped to make room.
