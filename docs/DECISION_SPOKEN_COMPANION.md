# Spoken accounts after a decision reveal

Open an ordinary demonstration or complete practice in Decision practice. After explicitly revealing the current step, optionally open its complete spoken-explanation notebook. Every original frame and retained audio file is admitted by the maintained notebook reader; the complete normalized demonstration must match, including its accounts, rather than only its clip or step IDs.

Play a current-step recording explicitly. It is a separately supplied account with its own clock, not original demonstration sound, verified expertise, or an automatic answer key. Pairing and recording metadata are unavailable before full reveal. Early reveal retains the existing assistance chronology.

Source, step, session, restart and hidden-page changes stop existing playback and retire pending admission. A refused replacement leaves the previously paired notebook available in the current revealed context. Stale callbacks cannot adopt a notebook or stop a current player. Practice exports remain unchanged and exclude the companion: retain its separate notebook and pair it again after reopening practice. No listening events, transcripts, scores or session schema are added.
