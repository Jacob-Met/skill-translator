# Inspect a guide relationship through intermediate states

A point can finish where it began yet depart from an intended guide in the middle. Save a complete **Sampled path** account with its original frames, then open **Inspect a fixed guide relationship**. The receiver preserves that path and every original PNG; it does not create another practice attempt.

Choose an observed selected moment as the reference. Its matched point supplies the original signed perpendicular offset. Choose two other retained marks in that same frame as the directed guide anchors, declare that they define a fixed line in the shared plane throughout the samples, and supply an offset-change tolerance with its basis. The reference calibration, point correspondence and common-plane assumptions remain those of the original path.

The original guide line is unbounded. Projection before or beyond either anchor stays explicit. A dashed segment in each calibrated source frame identifies the declared anchors; it does not claim that a moving physical tool was tracked. The selected point is outlined at its own retained source coordinate.

## What the result can establish

Each observed selected point is assessed with the maintained guide-alignment evaluator. The receiver projects the fixed reference anchors and that point into the evaluator temporarily; it does not save synthetic practice events or expose its unrelated point-placement score. Original source annotations and sampled-path geometry remain unchanged.

A sampled offset outside the declared tolerance is retained as a violation. A missing selected point is unobserved; coincident or numerically unresolved guide anchors keep the relationship unresolved. If all observed samples are within tolerance but one sample is missing, the combined result remains unresolved. A known violation remains a violation even if other samples are missing.

The criterion may be exploratory and chosen after viewing the source; this editable file does not authenticate when it was declared. Agreement at every selected sample does not prove that the intervening motion kept the relationship. The maintained guide evaluator's numerical guard is disclosed separately from the supplied tolerance. Neither is an estimate of calibration uncertainty, a contact measurement, a physical success judgment or evidence of learning.

## Original evidence and replay

The saved guide bundle retains the original sampled-path account and its PNG frames once, together with the reference moment, guide declaration and fixed-line confirmation. The maintained sequence/image receiver checks original PNG identity. On reopening, guide findings are recomputed from the supplied inputs; saved answers are not trusted. Invalid replacement inputs leave the accepted account intact. A newer declaration edit cancels an older pending read or save.

**Save original sampled path** exports that prior account explicitly. It does not silently rewrite or merge the guide criterion into it.

## Verification

`node test/path-guide.test.mjs` covers return-to-origin excursions, missing evidence, preserved parallel offset, beyond-anchor projection, reversed and vertical guides, degenerate anchors, source identity, declaration refusal and raw replay. `node tools/browser-path-guide.mjs` exercises the actual original-frame receiver, complete download/native reopen, malformed image refusal, stale-read cancellation and desktop/narrow keyboard controls. Fixtures are authored analytic source examples, not measured motion or a learning study.
