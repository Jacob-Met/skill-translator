# Locate appearance changes in scan samples

In the demonstration sequence's visual scan, expand **Locate changed appearance in these samples** for a compared pair. Analysis runs on first open. The earlier and later sampled images show the same magenta region bounds. The list retains all connected regions, including excluded regions smaller than four sampled pixels and regions touching the image boundary. Use the existing Inspect earlier/later controls to seek the original footage, then retain full-resolution moments normally.

The method reuses the maintained median additive RGB shift and 16-level residual-channel threshold. It constructs a binary mask of pixels whose largest absolute adjusted channel residual is at least 16. The pinned MICROVERSE `analyzePixels` implementation extracts eight-connected components from that mask, with a minimum area of four pixels. The mask is an intermediate calculation, not a source photograph. Its component intensity has no physical interpretation. Bounds use sampled-image coordinates with exclusive right and bottom edges.

This makes the location of changed sampled appearance inspectable where the prior scalar-only comparison supplied only magnitude. A moved isolated patch can yield both a departed region and an arrived region; these are not automatically matched as an object. Uniform brightening alone yields no adjusted regions while its raw score remains visible. Smaller changes are counted and listed as excluded. Samples smaller than 16 pixels on either side or a component-limit refusal produce an unavailable analysis. Missing, repeated, reversed or changed-dimension scan pairs never gain region overlays.

The fixed profile is not tuned to an expert action or craft. Lighting, camera movement, compression, occlusion and touching changes can create, erase or combine regions. Resizing can lose small detail. Uniform adjustment can erase a real global material change. Neither region location nor an empty mask establishes contact, completion, skill, or absence of movement between sampled frames. The automatic regions are separate from an observer's optional original-resolution fixed rectangle; they do not choose or modify that rectangle.

Verification commands: `node --test test/scan-change-regions.test.mjs test/visual-scan.test.mjs`, `node tools/verify-pixel-core.mjs`, and the maintained `tools/browser-visual-scan.mjs` with installed Playwright/Chrome. Authored raster and video controls do not establish physical-skill transfer or a human annotation-time benefit. Actual execution and independent review evidence must be recorded separately.
