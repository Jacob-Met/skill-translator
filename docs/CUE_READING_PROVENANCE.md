# Per-reading provenance

This separate account preserves why each categorical reading was supplied. Select an original cue, enter the category, and explicitly choose a hypothetical answer, observer source-frame inspection, or origin not established. Give each entry a basis. A source inspection additionally binds the retained example, original moment and PNG digest. The derived display supplies the original presented time and selected-interval role. A reported inspection does not verify the category or create an independent trial.

All entries still share the single declared total mismatch budget. Provenance does not change the comparison mathematics or silently remove hypothetical/unspecified entries. Original cue studies and prior repeated-reading accounts remain separate. The new account saves original inputs only and recomputes on reopen; forged frame anchors and missing provenance refuse before replacing accepted work.

Browser course: supply BROWSER_HELPER, CHROME_BINARY, QA_OUTPUT and SOURCE_BUNDLE explicitly to `node tools/browser-reading-provenance.mjs`; use a new writable output directory and ordinary sandbox-enabled Chrome.
