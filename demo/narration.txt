Skill Translator helps a bookbinding teacher move a marked position from one drawing to a sheet of a different size. It tells you how far off your placement is, in millimetres. On the left is the source drawing. It has three marks. Each mark follows a rule about where it sits. On the right is the target sheet, three hundred and twenty by one hundred and ninety millimetres. The red dot is where the mark should go. Now place your own mark. Select the target sheet, then nudge the ring with the arrow keys. Each press moves it one millimetre. Press Evaluate placement. The app checks your ring against the tolerance for this mark. The result line says whether you are within tolerance, and it gives the distance in millimetres. Next, look at the question behind the mark. What if the mark meant a different rule? Scroll down to find out. Choose another rule to compare. Here it is a fixed distance from the top edge. The app predicts where that rule would put the mark. Add a measurement from a second sheet size. Give the sheet size, where the mark sits, and how sure you are. Press Test against all five rules. Only the proportional rule still fits. The other cards show which rules this measurement rules out. That is Skill Translator. Place a mark, check it, then test the rule with a new measurement.
