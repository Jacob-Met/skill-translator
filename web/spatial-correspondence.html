Original source annotations

Use 3–7 reference points and at least as many points in the other source, up to 7. Every reference point must be matched; additional observed marks stay visible and are never discarded. Missing reference roles are not inferred. Separate positive stretch magnitudes along the reference axes are inferred jointly with the complete point mapping; no scale or pair list is required. Every pair must fit one shared combination of magnitudes within this bound in the other source’s millimetres. Signs and orientation are not inferred. The bound is exploratory; calibration uncertainty and semantic identity are not inferred. Previously saved fixed-distance, common-scale and equal-point axis proposals keep their original model.
