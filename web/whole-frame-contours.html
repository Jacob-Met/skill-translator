Inspect the complete original frame

Explicit whole-original-frame mode processes every original pixel in one native Canny invocation, up to 2,097,152 pixels. It does not stitch tile fields. These are image proposals, not identified physical boundaries or persistent objects.

Bounded ROI contour study

Removing artificial tile seams does not remove camera or detector uncertainty. Every native walk is retained, including nested and double-sided walks. Contours can omit interior edge pixels; the complete field remains available. An imported report is checked against retained evidence, not independently recomputed by this browser.
