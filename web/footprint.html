Add a patch alternative

The registration point is measured from the patch center in its unrotated axes: +x right, +y down. It must lie inside the declared patch. That point stays on the task mark when the patch rotates.

What the geometry leaves open

  • Patch dimensions and registration are declarations, not automatically measured tool geometry.
  • Pressure, friction, support stiffness, tool body, handle, operator reach and other obstacles are not represented.
  • Workpiece boundaries are not automatically forbidden zones. Signed clearances do not decide whether the operation can succeed.

Source, patch declarations and target setup are retained in the export. Results are recomputed on reopening. Nothing is uploaded.
