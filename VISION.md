# Skill Translator

## Purpose and audience

Help a craft expert preserve an explanation of a demonstrated task and help a
learner reason from its original evidence. The important relationship is between
the material, tool, intended result and feedback—not imitation of a wrist path.

The maintained product lives here. Sequence Studio captures local video moments,
retains original frames, and links before/after states to an authored account.
Source Studio calibrates photographed workpieces. Task-specific practice reuses
the retained account and keeps the learner's response distinct from the source.

## What great looks like

A teacher can keep the demonstration in sight while explaining a change, save
unfinished work, reopen it, and hand a useful lesson to a learner. A learner can
inspect the source and distinguish what was visible from what someone asserted.
A coordinate result is never presented as demonstrated physical mastery.

The source video is the primary working surface in strong alternatives:
[Kinovea's playback interface](https://www.kinovea.org/help/en/observation/playback_ui.html)
keeps playback, key images and annotation together;
[Dartfish](https://www.dartfish.com/features/) connects video inspection with
coaching feedback. Our advantage should be a clear, source-linked account of
decisions and adaptations. We do not yet match those products' mature video
navigation, capture integration or coaching delivery.

## Ranked open gaps

1. **Useful learner-facing lessons.** The existing offline lesson puts provenance
   before instruction and source images. Decision Practice also spends too much
   of the initial screen on setup; its comparison error should appear next to
   the evidence-reference choice. These are real observed journey gaps.
2. **Finding moments in real footage.** The visual scan completes on the observer's
   bookbinding clip, but its numerical interval list separates the user from the
   video. A source contact sheet with inspectable measurements is more useful
   than an expanding list of metrics.
3. **Demonstrated teaching value.** Local geometry and authored clips establish
   software behavior, not expert correctness, automatic extraction, physical
   success or skill transfer. Expert-authored material and unfamiliar learner
   trials must determine which capabilities are actually worth improving.
4. **Delivery and continuity.** Keep the downloadable runtime current with main.
   An explicit sequence export contains retained frames and accepted notes; it
   does not contain the original clip or every unfinished draft. No automatic
   cloud storage or service-side persistence is implied.
